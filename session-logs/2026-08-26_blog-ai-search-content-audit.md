# Blog AI Search Content Audit

## What was done

- Reviewed all 39 Markdown blog entries against Google's July 2026 guidance for succeeding in AI search.
- Inspected the shared blog route, content schema, generated output, citations, headings, body visuals, and topic overlap.
- Created `reports/2026-08-26-blog-ai-search-content-audit.md` with a per-post decision and phased rewrite roadmap.
- Made no changes to published blog content and did not deploy.

## Key findings or decisions

- The main weakness is commodity overlap and limited first-party evidence, not missing AI-specific markup.
- The current schema-for-AI article conflicts with Google's guidance and is a priority accuracy rewrite.
- Several website-necessity and web-designer-selection posts should be consolidated after checking Search Console performance.
- The content schema strips `lang`, `articleSection`, and `keywords`; the Chinese post currently renders as `en-NZ`.
- The Markdown website-cost article is shadowed by a standalone calculator at the same route.
