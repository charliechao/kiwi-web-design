Update your Google holiday hours before Christmas

When someone searches for your business over the holidays, Google shows your opening hours right next to your name. If those hours still say you're open on Christmas Day, you'll get customers turning up, or calling, to find you closed.

It takes about two minutes to fix, and Google lets you set special hours for specific dates without changing your normal ones.

How to do it:

  1. Search for your business name on Google while signed in to the account that manages your profile.
  2. Choose Edit profile, then look for Hours, and the option to add special hours (holiday hours).
  3. Add each date you'll be closed or running different hours. Think Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year's Day, and 2 January, plus any extra days you take off.
  4. Save. The change usually shows up within a day or so.

While you're at it, it's worth making sure your website says the same thing, so customers don't get two different answers.
