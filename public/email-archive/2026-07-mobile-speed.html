Why some visitors leave before your site even finishes loading

Picture someone standing at a job site, phone in hand, patchy reception, searching for a business like yours. If your website takes more than a couple of seconds to show up, most people won't wait. They'll hit back and call the next result instead.

This is separate from how a page is laid out. A site can look great and still lose customers because it's slow or awkward on a phone. A few common culprits we see on small business sites:

  1. Oversized images. A photo straight from a phone camera can be 5-10MB. On a slow connection, that alone can take several seconds to load.
  2. Too many bolted-on tools. Chat widgets, tracking scripts, and old plugins each add their own delay, and they add up.
  3. Menus and buttons too small to tap. If someone has to zoom in to press "Call Now," they may just give up.
  4. Forms that fight you on mobile. Tiny text fields, dropdowns that are hard to scroll, and no auto-fill support all cost you enquiries.

A simple test: turn off your wifi, use your phone's mobile data, and load your own website like a stranger would. Time how long it takes, and see how easy it is to actually tap through to call or enquire.
