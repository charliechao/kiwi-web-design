A five-minute website check before Christmas

The next few weeks are a funny time for a website. Some customers are rushing to get things sorted before the break, others are searching over the holidays and expecting a quick answer. Either way, your site is working while you're not.

Before things wind down, it's worth five minutes to check these:

  1. Opening hours and holiday dates. If you're closed or running reduced hours, say so on the site, not just on Facebook.
  2. Do your contact details actually work? Tap your phone number on your phone, and send yourself a test message through the contact form to make sure it lands in your inbox.
  3. Anything out of date. An old copyright year, last year's prices, an expired special, a "coming soon" that never came. Small things, but they make a business look less active than it is.
  4. Tell people when to expect a reply. A simple line like "We're away from 24 December and back on 12 January" stops an enquiry feeling ignored.
  5. Look at it on your phone. Most visitors will. If something looks off or is hard to tap, it's worth knowing before the new year.
