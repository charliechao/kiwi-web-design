The easiest way to get more Google reviews

Your website is built. The next job is making sure the people who find you on Google, and on Google Maps in particular, see a business other people trust.

Reviews are a big part of that. Most happy customers never leave one, and it's rarely because they don't want to. Nobody asked them, or the process felt like too much effort.

Three small habits make the biggest difference:

  • Ask at the right moment. Right after a job goes well, when the customer is happiest, not weeks later.
  • Send a direct link. In your Google Business Profile, choose the option to share a review link. One tap on a phone is far more likely to get done than "find us on Google".
  • Reply to every review. A short, genuine reply, including to the occasional less-than-perfect one, shows future customers that someone is paying attention.

Here's a message you can copy, paste into a text or email, and adjust to sound like you:

"Hi [name], thanks again for choosing us. If you're happy with how it went, a quick Google review would really help our small business. It only takes a minute: [your review link]. Cheers, [your name]"
