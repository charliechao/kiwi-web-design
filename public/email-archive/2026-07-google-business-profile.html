What shows up above your website on Google

When someone in your area searches for what you do, Google usually shows a map with three businesses before it shows any website results at all.

Those three spots don't come from your website. They come from your Google Business Profile — the free listing Google gives every business. Most businesses claim theirs, fill in half of it, and never touch it again.

Three things worth checking on yours this week:

  • Your primary category. Pick the most specific one available, not the broadest. This single field has an outsized effect on which searches you turn up in.
  • Recent photos. Profiles with photos added in the last few months get noticeably more clicks than ones with a logo and nothing else.
  • Review replies. Replying to reviews — good and bad — feeds into how Google ranks you locally, and customers read the replies as closely as the reviews.
