Do you know where your website traffic actually comes from?

Most business owners could tell you roughly how many people walk into their shop in a week. Far fewer could tell you how many people visited their website, where those visitors came from, or what they did once they got there.

That's not a website problem — it's a reporting problem. The data is already being collected. Most businesses just never had it set up properly, or never look at it.

Proper reporting answers three questions:

  • Where visitors come from. Google search, Google Maps, social media, other sites linking to you, or people typing your name in directly.
  • What they do once they land. Which pages they actually read, and whether they call, fill in a form, or leave without doing anything.
  • What's actually working. So effort and budget go toward what's bringing in enquiries, instead of guessing.
